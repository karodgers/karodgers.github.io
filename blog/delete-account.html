Rust Kisumu App Account Deletion

Account Deletion Process

Last Updated: 21 November, 2025

Data Deletion Policy

This document outlines the process for deleting your account and data from the Rust Kisumu App. We are committed to ensuring you have control over your personal information.

When you request to delete your account, the following data will be permanently removed from our systems:

  • Your account information (email address, profile details).
  • All your community contributions (posts, comments, replies, likes, dislikes, articles).
  • Meetup reservations and history.
  • Any other data associated with your account.

Please note that your account will be disabled immediately upon request, and all data will be permanently deleted within 90 days. This retention period is to allow for a grace period in case of accidental deletion requests and for our backup cycles to complete.

Steps to Delete Your Account

Follow these steps to permanently delete your account from the Rust Kisumu App.

1. Login to the Rust Kisumu App

Open the app and log in with your credentials (if not already logged in).

2. Navigate to the Profile Screen

Once logged in, go to your profile section and click on the 'Account Settings' option

3. Initiate Account Deletion

You will see the 'Delete Account' section - click on 'Delete Account' button (enter your password if already changed from google auth otherwise directly confirm account deletion).

4. Confirm Deletion

You will receive a confirm deletion message followed by a success message (if you choose to preed) that your account has been disabled and all your data will be deleted in 90 days.
